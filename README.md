### A. Introduction

## Money Heist (La Casa de Papel)

*Money Heist, also known as **La Casa de Papel**, is a Spanish crime thriller television series created by **Álex Pina**. It follows a group of criminals who work together to carry out carefully planned heists under the leadership of a mysterious man known as **the Professor**. The series combines crime, drama, suspense, and action, making it an exciting show for viewers who enjoy unexpected plot twists.*

- **Genre**: Crime, Thriller, Drama, Action
- **Release Year**: 2017
- **Country**: Spain
- **Creator**: Álex Pina

---

### B. Synopsis

Money Heist tells the story of a mysterious criminal mastermind known as the Professor, who recruits eight skilled individuals to carry out an ambitious robbery at the Royal Mint of Spain. Each member uses a city name as their identity to protect their real names and personal information.

Their main plan is to print billions of euros while keeping hostages inside the building. However, the operation becomes more complicated as the police begin investigating and negotiating with the robbers. Personal relationships, unexpected conflicts, and emotional decisions start affecting the group's carefully prepared plans.

As the story continues, the team faces several challenges that test their loyalty, intelligence, and teamwork. The series shows how even the most carefully planned operations can become unpredictable.

### C. Characters

#### Five Important Characters:
1. **The Professor** - The mastermind behind the heists who carefully plans every detail and guides the group.
2. **Berlin** - The Professor's brother and the leader inside the Royal Mint during the first heist.
3. **Tokyo** - One of the main members of the team who narrates much of the story and often acts impulsively.
4. **Rio** - A young hacker who helps the group with his technical knowledge and computer skills.
5. **Nairobi** - A skilled counterfeiter who is responsible for supervising the production of money.
